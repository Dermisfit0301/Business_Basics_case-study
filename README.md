🧾 Business Basics – IPL Case Study 
Welcome to the repository for the Business Basics Magazine Case Study, where data meets public interest. This project was developed as part of the Business Basics IPL Special and investigates the dual role of the Indian Premier League (IPL)—as a massive economic engine and a source of significant public health and social concerns.

📌 Project Overview
The IPL is more than just cricket—it's a ₹48,000+ Cr media juggernaut that fuels GDP growth, employment, and consumer engagement. But it also amplifies risky consumer behaviors through aggressive advertising from high-risk sectors like pan masala, gambling, and fantasy sports.

This case study dives into the hidden societal costs of IPL advertising and proposes ethical, data-driven solutions to drive responsible brand endorsements and mitigate harm.

🔍 Objectives
Quantify IPL’s economic contributions (media revenue, employment, GDP, taxes)

Assess harmful advertising impact (health, debt, social harm)

Evaluate celebrity endorsement ethics

Design solutions: ad regulation, accountability, tax incentives
